Remote support

Fixed from here, wherever you are

Most problems can be sorted without a visit. With your permission, we connect to your computer over the internet, see what you see and put it right while you watch, on Windows or Mac.

  1. 1

    Call or email

    Describe the problem and agree a time.

  2. 2

    Connect

    Open the support app and read out its one-time code. Nothing is left running afterwards.

  3. 3

    Sorted

    Watch it being fixed, ask questions, and end the session whenever you like.
